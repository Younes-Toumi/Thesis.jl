\section{Static versus Adaptive Design}
\label{sec:static_vs_dynamic}

The surrogate comparison of \ref{sec:surrogate_validation} established which metamodel provides the best accuracy under a static design. However, the question of \emph{where} those training points are placed is equally consequential. A passive \gls{lhs} distributes points uniformly across the augmented space regardless of the response structure, while an adaptive enrichment strategy concentrates evaluations in regions of high surrogate uncertainty. In the context of \gls{cabo}, the quality of the initial surrogate directly determines how many additional iterations the propagation loop requires before converging to accurate bounds: a better starting surrogate means fewer costly simulator calls inside \gls{cabo}. This section isolates that effect by comparing static and adaptive designs at identical total evaluation budgets, measuring both the surrogate accuracy achieved and the downstream effect on \gls{cabo} convergence.

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{subsec:static_dynamic_setup}

Two design strategies are compared under a common total budget of $n_{\max} = 50$ simulator evaluations:

\paragraph*{Static Sampling} A single design of $n_{\max}$ points is drawn and the surrogate is trained once on the full dataset. No sequential decisions are made and all evaluation locations are fixed before any simulator output is observed.

\paragraph*{Adaptive Sampling} An initial design of $n_0$ points is generated and the surrogate is trained. The remaining $n_{\max} - n_0$ evaluations are then placed sequentially using the active learning criterion appropriate to the surrogate, that is retrained after each new evaluation. The initial budget is set to $n_0 = 40$ for both the four-Gaussian function and $n_0 = 30$ for Ishigami, consistent with the $10 \cdot n_w$ rule of thumb used throughout.\\

The same design seed is used for the initial design of both strategies, so that any difference in final accuracy is attributable to the sequential placements rather than to the starting configuration.
